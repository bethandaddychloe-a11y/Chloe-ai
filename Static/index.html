      Scalp bot

      Uses the same strategies and rules as the main signal, on a fast chart, with a tight stop capped by volatility, small fixed targets, and two extra filters: enough indicator confirmations and the London and New York session (Bitcoin is always on). It gives signals only and never places trades.

      How the bot decides

      1. Runs the smart money, liquidity sweep, support and resistance, trend, breakout and line-chart strategies on the fast chart, in that order, and takes the first with a setup.
      2. Rejects it if the stop is wider than your ATR limit, if fewer than your chosen number of the 4 indicator confirmations agree, or if the higher timeframe trend is against it.
      3. Replaces the targets with fixed 1R, 1.5R and 2.2R so that each trade is quick, and charges 0.1R per trade for spread when it replays history.
      4. On your own server it also sends these to Telegram, at most a few per pair per day with a cooldown.

      Scalping costs more in spread than any other style, and the Exness spread on your account can be wider than the cost assumed here. Test on demo first.

      How trades are entered
      
        
      
        
      
        
      Adding to winning trades
      
        
      When a trade is in profit and its stop has moved to break-even, a fresh signal in the same direction can add a smaller position. You are asked first, the same way. Never more than the original risk is on the table, and it never adds to a losing or unprotected trade.
      
        
      
        
      
        
      Each further add needs another R of profit (1R, then 2R). The Expert Advisor moves a trade's stop to break-even at 1R, which is what makes an add possible.

      How the link works
      
    
      There is no password box on purpose. Your MT5 password would sit in a web page, and MT5 has no public login for a page like this. Instead your password goes to a bridge service (MetaApi) on its own site, and your Chloe server reads the account through that bridge with a read-only investor password.
      
    
        
      
      1. In your Exness Personal Area, open the account's settings and set an investor password. It can view but cannot trade.
        2. 
      
      2. Create a MetaApi account and add your MT5 login, server and the investor password there. Copy the account ID and your API token.
        3. 
      
      3. On your Render service, add the settings METAAPI_TOKEN and METAAPI_ACCOUNT_ID, then redeploy.
        4. 
      
      4. Open your Chloe server page, come back here and tap Check connection. The balance and open trades appear above.
        5. 
    
      
    
      Check MetaApi's current free limits and Exness terms yourself. I could not test the live link from here.
